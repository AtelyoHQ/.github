# Atelyo

We build software that does real work for people, and that they can trust to do it.

## Ion

A personal AI assistant that runs on your computer (Mac today, Windows next) and actually does the work, by voice or text.

- **Email and calendar:** reads, triages and drafts your email; manages your calendar.
- **Meeting notes:** listens quietly on calls and writes notes with who said what and the action items.
- **Coding:** hands bugs to a coding agent that works on its own branch, runs the tests in a sandbox and returns one clean commit to review.
- **Research:** looks things up on the web and shows its sources.
- **Your apps and screen:** works in apps on screen, points at things when you ask "where is…", and connects to Notion, Linear, Zapier, GitHub and more.
- **Routines:** "check my calendar for clashes every weekday at 9", plus a morning briefing.
- **Languages:** English, Hindi and Hinglish.

**Built to be trusted.** Every action goes through a safety layer: reads happen straight away, sends wait in an undo window, and deleting or paying always needs your yes. Ion checks that something worked before it says "done", and everything it does is logged.

**Built to be fast.** Everyday commands like "open Chrome" run in about 50 ms without calling a model; everything else goes to the smallest model that can do it well.

**Measured, not guessed.** Every change is checked against 207 automated tests and a benchmark of 225 real-world scenarios, the way compilers are benchmarked.

Ion is in private development. A beta is coming soon.

## Research

We're exploring **digital twins for lung cancer**: patient-specific models of tumour growth and treatment response, built with scientific machine learning (differential-equation models from the SciML ecosystem) and fitted to real patient cohorts.
